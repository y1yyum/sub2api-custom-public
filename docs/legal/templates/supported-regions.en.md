# Supported Countries and Regions Policy

**Last Updated:** November 1, 2026  
**Effective Date:** November 1, 2026

This Supported Countries and Regions Policy ("Policy") sets forth the geographic availability, international sanctions compliance, and territorial service boundaries governing access to **ai2e** ("we", "us", or "our"), accessible at **https://web.ai2e.cc**.

---

## 1. Scope & Core Territorial Policy

As an API routing and gateway platform for artificial intelligence models, ai2e operates strictly in authorized international jurisdictions and enforces stringent geographic access controls.

**ai2e does not provide services, account registration, API access, or customer support to individuals, organizations, or entities located in, or citizens or residents of, excluded and sanctioned territories.**

---

## 2. Excluded and Sanctioned Jurisdictions

### 2.1 Specific Territorial Exclusions
Access to ai2e is **strictly prohibited** from the following territories:

- **Mainland China**
- **Russia**
- **Iran**
- **Cuba**
- **North Korea (DPRK)**
- **Syria**
- **Crimea, Donetsk, and Luhansk regions**
- Any other territory subject to comprehensive bilateral or multilateral trade sanctions, export control embargoes, or specific operational exclusions established by ai2e.

### 2.2 Restricted Persons and Entities
Access to the Services is also strictly prohibited for:
1. Any individual or legal entity appearing on international sanctions watchlists, including the United States Specially Designated Nationals (SDN) list, the European Union Consolidated Financial Sanctions list, or United Nations Security Council sanctions lists.
2. Any entity owned, directly or indirectly, 50 percent or more by one or more blocked persons or entities.
3. Any party attempting to act as an agent, intermediary, or proxy for individuals or entities in the excluded regions listed above.

### 2.3 Strict Prohibition on Geo-Obfuscation
Users are expressly forbidden from utilizing Virtual Private Networks (VPNs), residential proxies, Tor exit nodes, spoofed IP routing, or any other technical mechanism to conceal their true geographic origin or bypass ai2e's territorial controls. 

Any account detected accessing the Services from **Mainland China, Russia, Iran**, or other excluded territories will be **immediately and permanently terminated**, all associated API keys revoked, and remaining balances forfeited without refund.

## 3. Upstream AI Model Provider Regional Alignment

### 3.1 Upstream Regional Restrictions
ai2e routes requests to third-party foundation models. Different upstream AI providers (e.g., OpenAI, Anthropic, Google DeepMind) enforce their own independent geographic restrictions and availability schedules:

- **OpenAI:** [Supported Countries and Territories](https://platform.openai.com/docs/supported-countries)
- **Anthropic (Claude):** [Supported Countries and Regions](https://www.anthropic.com/supported-countries)
- **Google Cloud / Gemini:** [Gemini API Available Regions](https://ai.google.dev/available_regions)

### 3.2 No-Bypass Disclaimer
- ai2e acts solely as a technical gateway and **does not bypass sovereign regulations or upstream provider geo-blocks.**
- If an upstream vendor imposes regional restrictions or compliance blocks on specific countries, ai2e does not guarantee connectivity or fallback for that model in restricted locations.
- We disclaim all liability for request failures, rate limiting, or connection severed by upstream regional firewalls.

### 4.1 International Infrastructure

You are independently responsible for ensuring that your access to and use of ai2e complies with all local laws and regulations applicable in your authorized territory.
