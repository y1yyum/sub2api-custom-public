# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** November 1, 2026  
**Effective Date:** November 1, 2026

**ai2e** ("we", "us", or "our") operates the web console, developer APIs, and routing platform accessible at **https://web.ai2e.cc** (collectively, the "Services").

We are committed to protecting your privacy and providing transparent information regarding how data is handled. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, process, disclose, and safeguard technical information, user data, and API request traffic when you use the Services.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Account Information
When you create an account on ai2e, we collect:
- **Registration Identifiers:** Email address, username (if provided), and cryptographic password hashes.
- **Authentication Data:** Multi-factor authentication metadata (TOTP registration timestamps, Passkey public key credentials).
- **Account Metadata:** Registration date, account status, assigned user groups, and subscription tier.

### 1.2 API Request & Usage Metadata
When you access the API via developer keys, our routing servers automatically log technical metadata necessary for metering, billing, rate limiting, and network security:
- Request timestamp and latency (response duration).
- Client IP address and HTTP User-Agent.
- Model name requested, endpoint path, and HTTP response code.
- Input token count, output token count, and total billed units.
- Error codes and routing channel identifiers.

### 1.3 Billing & Payment Data
- If you make payments via third-party processors (e.g., Stripe) or digital asset networks (e.g., USDT / crypto gateways), we store transaction references, top-up amounts, timestamps, and currency identifiers.
- **We do not collect or store full credit card numbers, CVVs, or private crypto keys.** All payment processing is handled directly by PCI-DSS compliant or specialized third-party payment gateways.

---

## 2. Processing of Prompts and Model Outputs

### 2.1 Ephemeral Proxying & Transmission
- **Real-Time Proxying:** When you send an API request (including prompts, conversation histories, system instructions, and files), ai2e acts as a real-time gateway that securely forwards your request over encrypted TLS connections to the appropriate upstream model provider (e.g., OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, or DeepSeek).
- **Ephemeral Streaming:** Responses generated by the models are streamed back to your client in real time.
- **No Model Training:** ai2e **does not use your prompts, conversation data, or generated completions to train or fine-tune artificial intelligence models.**

### 2.2 Content Storage vs. Metadata Logging
- By default, our core database records **only billing and operational metadata** (tokens, model, timestamp, response status).
- If debug logging or payload caching is enabled by the platform administrator for diagnostic or auditing purposes, such logs are subject to strict role-based access controls and automatic expiration/purge policies.

### 2.3 Privacy Safeguards on Upstream Accounts
Where supported by our architecture and upstream agreements (e.g., dedicated enterprise API endpoints or privacy-enabled OAuth channels with training opt-outs enabled), requests are routed through channels configured to prevent upstream model training on customer inputs.

---

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We process collected information for the following legitimate business purposes:
1. **Service Delivery:** Routing API requests, balancing traffic across channels, and delivering model outputs.
2. **Billing & Accounting:** Calculating token usage, managing account balances, processing subscription renewals, and generating transaction receipts.
3. **Security & Abuse Prevention:** Enforcing rate limits, detecting brute-force attacks, mitigating DDoS threats, and identifying violations of our [Acceptable Use Policy](/legal/usage-policy).
4. **Maintenance & Optimization:** Diagnosing server errors, monitoring channel latency, and improving gateway routing efficiency.
5. **Customer Support:** Resolving issues submitted through our in-platform Support Ticket System.

## 4. Territorial Exclusions & International Processing

### 4.1 Excluded Regions
ai2e does not provide services, solicit customers, or knowingly collect personal information from individuals residing in **Mainland China, Russia, Iran**, or other sanctioned territories. If we discover that an account has been established from an excluded jurisdiction, that account and associated data will be suspended and deleted in accordance with our policies.

### 4.2 Cross-Border Transfers
By submitting API requests to ai2e, you acknowledge that your Inputs are forwarded to third-party model inference clusters which may be located in jurisdictions including the United States, the European Union, Japan, and other locations where upstream providers operate.

We share information only in the limited circumstances described below:

### 5.1 Upstream Model Providers
To execute your API requests, your Inputs (prompts, context, images, or documents) are transmitted to the third-party model vendor powering the specific model endpoint you selected. Each upstream provider processes data in accordance with their respective privacy and enterprise data policies:
- OpenAI, LLC
- Anthropic, PBC
- Google LLC / Google Cloud
- DeepSeek Technologies
- Other upstream providers configured on the platform

### 5.2 Infrastructure & Service Providers
We may utilize trusted third-party service providers who assist in operating the platform:
- Cloud hosting, database, and content delivery network (CDN) providers.
- Transaction and payment processing providers.
- Transactional email providers for password resets and verification notices.

These providers have access only to the information necessary to perform their specific services and are obligated to maintain confidentiality.

### 5.3 Legal Compliance & Protection
We may disclose information if required to do so by applicable law, court order, or governmental authority, or when we believe in good faith that disclosure is necessary to protect the rights, property, or safety of ai2e, our users, or the public.

## 6. Data Security

We implement industry-standard safeguards to protect your personal information and API keys:
- **Encryption in Transit:** All traffic between your client, our gateway, and upstream providers is encrypted using Modern TLS (Transport Layer Security) protocols.
- **Credential Protection:** Passwords and sensitive secrets are stored using robust cryptographic hashing algorithms. API keys are masked or hashed in administrative interfaces.
- **Access Controls:** Access to production infrastructure and databases is restricted to authorized personnel on a least-privilege basis.

---

## 7. Data Retention & Deletion

- **Account Data:** Retained for as long as your account remains active. If you request account closure through a support ticket, your personal information will be deleted or anonymized, except where retention is required by law or legitimate accounting requirements.
- **API Audit Logs:** Request metadata (IP addresses, token counts, timestamps) is retained for operational auditing and billing reconciliation for a period defined by administrative settings (typically up to 180 days), after which it is automatically purged or aggregated.
